Knollwood Design Company creates handmade sewn goods and related crafts in small batches.

The goal is simple: make useful, giftable pieces that feel more personal than something pulled from a warehouse shelf.

Because pieces are handmade and often produced in limited runs, fabrics, colors, and seasonal designs can change throughout the year.
